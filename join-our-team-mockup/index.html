Good to know

Questions people ask before applying

Do you take on apprentices?

Yes. We take on first to fourth year apprentices across plumbing and gas, air conditioning, electrical, roofing and renovations. You earn while you learn and train on real jobs, with our in-house training wall and the Pathways program behind you.

Do I need my licence and my own tools?

Bring a current licence for your trade where it applies, a driver's licence, and a good attitude. Let us know in your application what you have, and we will talk through the rest.

Where would I be working?

All Adelaide metro suburbs. We are based at Stepney and work across the metro area.

What happens after I apply?

We read every application. If it looks like a fit, one of our team will get in touch to have a chat. If there is nothing open for you right now, we keep your details on file so we can reach out when something comes up.

I am not sure which role suits me.

Pick the closest one and tell us the rest in your message. Plenty of our people started in a role that was not their final one.

Do you hire people without trade experience?

For apprenticeships and labouring, yes. We are looking for reliability and willingness to learn as much as anything.
