Only 8 members had a 2nd fresh engagement across the full 22-month window. Median gap: ~200 days.

Why the gap? A member's call-out generates ~12 billed jobs on average — investigation, install, return visits — so one visit covers a lot of ground before they need us again. Non-members average ~3 billed jobs per call-out, so their next need comes fresh sooner.

Members are worth twice as much — and they don't come back any faster.

What counts as a 'new job': a fresh engagement is a genuinely new call-out — not a follow-on from a prior quote, not a recall child of an earlier job. This removes the investigation-then-dispatch double-count that inflates raw job counts. The CLV report uses the same definition throughout.

The opportunity

Members are the business's most valuable customers. They pay more. They return more often on a billing basis. But on the metric that matters — when will they genuinely need us next? — they don't re-engage any faster than non-members.

That gap is addressable. A timely, value-first email sequence could be the difference between a member booking at month 3 vs month 7. Even a small directional lift on a cohort spending $9.7 million a year is worth testing.

If the emails work at all, the path from email to SMS to full evergreen nurture is clear. If they don't — we know before over-investing.

The highest-value customers are the ones most worth a light, timely nudge to come back sooner.

Why value first

The sequence follows a simple principle: give away the secrets, sell the implementation. Each email leads with genuinely useful content from our own blogs — the DIY fix, the health check, the warning signs — before it mentions anything we sell.

The member difference is the hook: you could do this yourself, or as a member you call 08 7323 1253 and it's handled. Member pricing. Coupons. Warranty. The dream outcome — a home that's looked after — is already paid for. The emails just teach members to use it.

"You don't have a sales problem or a marketing problem. You have an offer problem." — Alex Hormozi, $100M Offers. Our offer is membership; these emails make the offer felt.

The MVP test

  • The Day-0 welcome email goes to 1,283 consent-gated members — the simplest version of the flow, tested first
  • 50/50 A/B split — flow gets the email, control held out (attribution, not significance)
  • Recipient list from ServiceTitan, consent-gated (email consent ON, doNotMail false)
  • Measured: fresh booking within 90 days vs control — directional read, re-engagement tracked open-ended

Validate the channel with one email. Then the full five-email sequence becomes the always-on onboarding every new member receives.

What we'll learn

This MVP isn't a revenue commitment. It's validated learning on the smallest possible test.

If it moves

Turn on the full five-email sequence. Extend to SMS, seasonal campaigns, and service-type nurture — with confidence the channel works.

If it doesn't

We know before over-investing. The infrastructure — Brevo, n8n, A/B rig, consent write-back — is built and reusable.

Either way: a clean baseline, a directional read, and the plumbing in place for whatever comes next.
