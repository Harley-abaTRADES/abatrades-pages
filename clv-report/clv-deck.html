Slide 2 · What we found

  • Members are 13% of customers — but 48% of revenue
  • Member AOV: $5,699 vs non-member $2,892 (2x)
  • But beneath the headline: investigation + dispatch inflates 'orders' 3.9x
  • The honest picture — using fresh engagements only — is much starker
The CLV report headline numbers. Establish the value asymmetry, then pivot to the gap.

Slide 3 · Re-engagement reality

  • 6-month fresh-engagement rate: 4.2% (1 in 24 members start a new job per half-year)
  • Median gap between genuine new jobs: ~200 days
  • Only 8 members out of 1,316 had a 2nd fresh engagement in 22 months
  • Members are our most valuable customers — and they barely come back on their own
The bridge slide. This is the problem statement that justifies the initiative.

Slide 4 · The opportunity

  • Members worth ~2x more, but re-engage once every ~7 months
  • No natural cadence pushing them back to us
  • A light, timely email touch could pull that forward
  • Goal: move the 4.2% 6-month rate directionally upward
Framing the asymmetry as the opportunity. Don't overclaim — the trial is small, directional.

Slide 5 · This week

  • Plain-text post-purchase email to 1,283 members with valid email
  • 50/50 A/B split — flow gets email, control held out (attribution, not significance)
  • Built on Brevo for delivery + unsubscribe handling
  • Recipient list from ServiceTitan, consent-gated, Humm excluded
The execution plan. Concise — this is what's happening right now.

Slide 6 · Costs

  • Brevo: free tier (300 emails/day, covers the 1,283-member cohort comfortably)
  • n8n: self-hosted cloud instance ~$20–40/month (workflow automation — trigger logic, contact sync, unsubscribe webhook)
  • ServiceTitan API: already provisioned, no incremental cost
  • Total MVP run cost: under $100 for the trial period
Keep costs real and low — this is not a big-budget initiative. Brevo is free. n8n is the only line item.

Slide 7 · How it works

  • One email, plain text, post-purchase timing
  • "Hi [first name], thanks for choosing aba TRADES. As a Gold member, here's what to know about your next call…"
  • One CTA: call or book online. No upsell, no 10%-off-now — just a reminder they're a member
  • Measured: booking within 90 days vs control (directional), open/click as leading indicator
Creative concept. Paint the picture — what the member actually receives.

Slide 8 · What we'll learn

  • Does a single email move the needle on re-engagement? (90-day fresh-booking rate)
  • If yes: scale to SMS, multi-step nurture flows, service-type targeting
  • If no, or unclear: the MVP was the right size test; we know before over-investing
  • Either way: we'll have a clean baseline, a directional read, and the infrastructure built
Close with the learning agenda. This isn't a revenue-commitment slide — it's about validated learning.
