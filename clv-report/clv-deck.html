Across the full 22-month window since Nov 2024, only 8 members out of 1,316 had a second fresh engagement. The median gap between a member's new engagements is ~200 days.

On a fresh-engagement basis, membership does not create a re-engagement advantage. Members are worth twice as much — and they don't come back any faster.

What counts as a 'new job' here: a job is a fresh engagement — a genuinely new call-out — only when it is not a follow-on visit from a prior quote, and not a recall child of an earlier job. This removes the investigation-then-dispatch double-count that inflates raw job counts. The CLV report uses the same definition throughout.

The opportunity

Members are the business's most valuable customers. They pay more. They return more often on a billing basis. But on the metric that matters — when will they genuinely need us next? — they don't re-engage any faster than non-members.

That gap is addressable. A light, timely email touch post-purchase could be the difference between a member booking at month 3 vs month 7. Even a small directional lift on a cohort spending $9.7 million a year is worth testing.

If the email works at all, the path from email to SMS to evergreen nurture flows is clear. If it doesn't — we know before over-investing.

The MVP

  • Plain-text post-purchase email to 1,283 members with valid email
  • 50/50 A/B split — flow gets email, control held out (attribution, not significance)
  • Built on Brevo (free tier) for delivery + unsubscribe handling
  • Recipient list from ServiceTitan, consent-gated
  • Measured: booking within 90 days vs control (directional)

How it works

One email. Plain text. Sent after a member's qualifying job completes — timed to land when the memory of the service is fresh but before the next need arises.

The email's job is simple: be the reason they think of us first next time something breaks. No upsell. No discount. Just a reminder that they're a member and we're here.

Copy and flow design in progress — Josh is working on this.

Open and click-through give us a leading indicator in days. Fresh bookings within 90 days — compared to the held-out control group — tell us whether it worked.

What we'll learn

This MVP isn't a revenue commitment. It's validated learning on the smallest possible test.

If it moves

Scale to SMS, multi-step nurture flows, service-type targeting, and evergreen sequences — with confidence the channel works

If it doesn't

We know before over-investing. The infrastructure — Brevo, n8n, A/B rig — is built and reusable

Either way: a clean baseline, a directional read, and the plumbing in place for whatever comes next.
