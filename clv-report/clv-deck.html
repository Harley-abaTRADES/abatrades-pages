The opportunity

Members are worth roughly double the revenue of non-members, yet they re-engage at just 4.2% over six months. There is no natural cadence pulling them back to us.

A light, timely email touch — post-purchase, plain text, no hard sell — could pull that forward. Even a small directional lift on a cohort spending $9.7 million a year is worth testing.

If we can move the needle at all, the path from email to SMS to multi-step nurture is clear. If not, we know before over-investing.

How it works

One email. Plain text. Post-purchase timing. No upsell, no discount code, no heavy design — just a reminder that they're a member.

"Hi {{first_name}}, thanks for choosing aba TRADES for your recent {{service_type}} job. As a Gold member, you get priority service, discounted rates, and peace of mind on every call-out. When you next need us — call or book online and we'll be there. — aba TRADES"

The email's job is simple: be the reason they think of us first. Open and click-through give us a leading indicator in days; booking rate within 90 days tells us whether it worked.
