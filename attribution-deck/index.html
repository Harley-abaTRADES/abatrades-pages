The three things we want to know

Channel
Where the customer came from: paid search, organic, Google listing, email, print, social.
Surface
How they got in touch: a phone call, a website form, a chat.
Revenue
The real job money that landed, mapped back to when it came.

Channel and surface are different facts. A phone call is not a channel, it is how the call happened; the channel is what drove it.

03 · What is missing

Three things stand in the way

The ad click ID is lost
GCLID lives in the click URL for only about 90 days and is not captured anywhere today. Once lost, it is gone forever. This is the signal that would name the exact ad.
not captured
The number map is hand-built
Campaigns come from a manually-maintained number-to-campaign table in ServiceTitan. It mis-files office/hotline calls and some numbers map unevenly. No independent check confirms it.
untrusted today
No revenue flows back to Google
Google Ads and GA4 see how many clicks and calls happen, but not which jobs paid out. So bidding optimises on lead counts, not on the money each ad earns.
no conversion value

04 · How it lands

The connected dots

From a completed job back to Google, in six steps.

1
Capture the click identifier. The GCLID is grabbed at the click: on a web form by the first-party snippet, on a phone call by CallRail's DNI.
2
Persist onto the lead. The identifier is written to the ServiceTitan lead at enquiry.
3
Close the job, earn the money. The job completes and earns real invoiced revenue in ServiceTitan.
4
The cron reads the joins. Revenue-sync reads the completed job, its GCLID, its campaign and its contact.
5
Upload via the Data Manager API. A valued conversion keyed by GCLID goes to Google Ads; a revenue event keyed by client_id goes to GA4.
6
Google matches server-side. Ads credits the exact click so Smart Bidding optimises on money, and GA4 attributes the revenue to the channel via source/medium.
05 · What we are proposing

Break the taxonomy apart to put it back correctly

Split channel from surface
Rename the 73 campaign buckets onto a clean source/medium axis (google_cpc, google_organic, google_gmb, meta_cpc, sms, print), moving the contact means (call, form, chat) out of the campaign name into its own field.
Add the capture layer
A first-party snippet for web leads and CallRail DNI for phone calls, so every enquiry carries the GCLID that produced it.
Close the loop to Google
CRON 1 sends real invoiced revenue back as conversion value in Google Ads and revenue-by-channel in GA4, so bidding and reporting run on money, not lead counts.
