The three things we want to know

Channel
Where the customer came from: paid search, organic, Google listing, email, print, social.
Surface
How they got in touch: a phone call, a website form, a chat.

03 · What is missing

Three things stand in the way

The ad click ID is lost
GCLID lives in the click URL for only about 90 days and is not captured anywhere today. Once lost, it is gone forever. This is the signal that would name the exact ad.
not captured
The number map is hand-built
Campaigns come from a manually-maintained number-to-campaign table in ServiceTitan. It mis-files office/hotline calls and some numbers map unevenly. No independent check confirms it.
untrusted today
The call's source is not stored on the job
ServiceTitan shows the caller and the number they dialed, but the source that produced the call (the channel, the ad click) is not written against the job. That is the line we need to complete.
not mapped

04 · How it lands

The connected dots

From a captured call to the source on the job, in four steps, with Google conversion value as a separate line still under investigation.

1
Capture the call source. WildJar stores the caller, the tracking number, the channel (source/medium), the GCLID if there is one, and the call time.
2
Map it onto the job. A script on Harley's box matches each call to its ServiceTitan job by phone number (call time as a check) and pulls the stored source.
3
Store on the job. The source row (channel, keyword, campaign, GCLID) is written onto the ServiceTitan job so the system of record holds the full picture.
4
Pair the data. ServiceTitan (and our dashboard) reads each job's revenue against its channel. ServiceTitan pairs the data in its own dashboard, the same way we already do.

The separate line

5
Conversion value into Google. Pushing job revenue back into Google Ads and GA4 as conversion value is a separate question. ServiceTitan does not appear to do it in Australia today, so we are still investigating the path (initially via the Google Ads account link, or a native tracker upload).
05 · Web enquiries

Forms and chat are tracked too

Not every job arrives by phone. Web form submissions and live chat carry their own source, and we capture both so enquiries can be tied back to the CRM once they become jobs.

Web forms today
Gravity Forms submissions currently land in the admin inbox as emails, not straight into ServiceTitan. Where a form has hidden tracking fields wired, Harley's box already polls the form feed (Gravity Forms REST) every ~15 minutes and pulls each submission with its GCLID and source/medium.
Live chat (Podium)
Website chat and SMS come through Podium as lead records and carry their own channel signal back to the job.
The Argon work
We've asked Argon (our site developer) to wire the visitor's GA4 client id and source onto every form submission, starting with aba ELECTRICAL, so each enquiry carries the tracking value into the CRM. Argon is scoping a clean, reusable solution across our sites.
06 · The build

Who does what

Call-tracking platforms capture and store the call's source. They do not push ServiceTitan revenue anywhere by themselves. Mapping that source onto the job is the work in the middle, and it is ours to do.

WildJar (call tracking)
Stores the phone call source data: caller, tracking number, channel, GCLID, time. Australian, local support, and the vendor ServiceTitan's own call-attribution product is built around. Recommended over CallRail for the ST path.
Harley's script (the mapping)
Joins each captured call to its ServiceTitan job by phone number and writes the stored source onto the job. This is the mapping-in step, done on our side, that ServiceTitan does not do for call source.
ServiceTitan (the system of record)
Holds jobs, revenue and the source data we store onto each. Its own marketing tool pairs the data in its own dashboard, but it does not appear able to push job revenue value back into GA4 or Google Ads in Australia today.
07 · What we are proposing

Break the taxonomy apart to put it back correctly

Split channel from surface
Rename the 73 campaign buckets onto a clean source/medium axis (google_cpc, google_organic, google_gmb, meta_cpc, sms, print), moving the contact means (call, form, chat) out of the campaign name into its own field.
Add the capture layer
WildJar DNI for phone calls and a first-party snippet for web leads, so every enquiry carries the source and GCLID that produced it. Store that source against the ServiceTitan job via our mapping script.
Investigate conversion value to Google
Separately investigate pushing job revenue into Google Ads and GA4 as conversion value, initially via the Google Ads account link or a native tracker upload. Not committed yet; it is a distinct workstream from the source-data mapping.
